## Our standard

We welcome participation regardless of experience, identity, background, or
location. Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather
than people, accept constructive feedback, and protect private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `paul.sczurek@gmail.com`. Maintainers will
review reports in confidence, consider context and impact, and may edit or
remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban an
individual.

Maintainers must apply these standards fairly and avoid conflicts of interest.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a
violation.
